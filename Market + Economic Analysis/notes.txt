	Federal income tax is heavily concentrated at the top of the income scale, with the top 1% of earners paying the 
	largest share of total federal income tax. In 2021, the top 10% of earners paid about 76% of all federal income 
	taxes, while the top 50% paid nearly 98%. The top 1% paid 45.8% of all individual income taxes in 2023, and a high-income 
	group with an Adjusted Gross Income (AGI) between $100,000 and $1 million paid about 54% of the total in 2020.
	
	The SS&C GlobeOp Forward Redemption Indicator is a monthly report from SS&C Technologies that measures the percentage of 
	investor redemption notices received for hedge funds on its platform. It serves as a gauge of investor sentiment, with a 
	higher percentage indicating more investors are preparing to pull their money out. The indicator is calculated as the sum 
	of forward redemption notices divided by the assets under administration and is published on the 15th business day of the 
	month.
